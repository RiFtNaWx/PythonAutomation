# RS29511 I2C-buffer DC Path B

## Why Soo wrap was wrong

Repo-root `logic_tests.test_supply_current` / `test_output_voltage` / `test_cap_load` are generic CMOS (one VCC, unknown pin, IDD in mA). PDF 7.5 ICC is 2.0 typ / 4.5 mA max at VCC=5.5, SDAIN=SCLIN=0, 10k on OUT. READY is open-drain. Cio SDA/SCL is 6.5/10 pF.

Do not dump rs29511 into eugene CMOS IDD (1.65 V corner, VIN=5.5).

## Body

- File: `ate/tests/logic/rs29511_dc.py` -- `run_icc` / `run_vout` / `run_cap`. Does not `register`.
- `wraps.py` routes rs29511 those three ids here.
- ICC: AWG CH1/CH2 DC 0 V, PSU CH1 2.3/3.3/5.0/5.5, OVP 5.6, DMM DCI, stamp `ICC_uA` at 5.5 V. `hotswap_dc=True`.
- READY: EN=VCC jumper, 10k pull-up, DMM VOLT, stamp `VOUT_V`.
- Cio: PSU OFF, DMM CAP SDAIN, stamp `CAP_pF`.
- Limits: `ICC_uA` max 4500 (4.5 mA), `CAP_pF` max 10.

## SIM leftover (named, not faked)

SIM `_icc_a` is CMOS ~0.6 uA at 5.5 V. Do not bump global SIM current to 2 mA -- that SPEC-FAILs CMOS IDD max 1 uA. Physics map why: "SIM CMOS uA not 2.0/4.5 mA PDF". USB SCPI path is the same.

SIM CAP is 5 pF (rs0302 Cioff max 6). PDF typ 6.5 leftover-named.
